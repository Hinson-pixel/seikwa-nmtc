Our Heritage

Training Tomorrow's Healthcare Heroes in the Bono Region

Established to address the growing demand for highly skilled healthcare personnel within the Tain District and the country at large, Seikwa Nursing and Midwifery Training College stands as a beacon of academic rigor and professional discipline.

We pride ourselves on offering comprehensive clinical instruction, standard practical nursing laboratories, and an environment that builds strong moral character, empathy, and professional excellence.

Our Vision

To become a premier healthcare training institution recognized globally for producing innovative, disciplined, and compassionate nursing and midwifery practitioners.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality healthcare education through evidence-based training, modern technology, and collaborative healthcare workflows to support community wellbeing.
